id: REQ-FS-005
title: All loops have a statically determinable upper bound
type: safety
status: accepted
verification: analysis
---

## Statement

Every loop in the runtime library and in code generated by the zserio-c
generator SHALL have an upper iteration bound that is determinable from
constants and inputs available at the start of the loop. Reading a
length-prefixed field SHALL bound the subsequent loop by that length;
reading an unbounded stream SHALL be done with an explicit per-call
maximum supplied by the caller.

## Rationale

Bounded loops are the foundation of WCET analysis. They are required by
MISRA C:2012 Rule 14.2 (in spirit) and by every static-timing analysis
tool we expect to use. They also harden the decode path against
maliciously or accidentally malformed inputs that would otherwise cause
the codec to hang or to allocate without limit.

## Verification

- cppcheck `--addon=misra` and code review.
- A negative test set submits crafted inputs whose declared lengths are
  beyond a configured maximum and asserts that the decoder returns the
  appropriate error status without executing further iterations.

## Notes

- The "configured maximum" is a per-call parameter, not a global. This
  prevents one caller's policy choice from constraining another's, and
  it keeps the runtime itself free of policy.
